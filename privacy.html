AniBinge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

AniBinge ("the app") is an unofficial, third-party client for AniList that helps you track the anime you watch. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. AniBinge is developed by Sunehar Sandhu. Questions: sunehar.sandhu@gmail.com.

Summary: AniBinge does not run its own servers that store your data, does not sell your data, shows no ads, and performs no cross-app tracking. Your anime list lives on your AniList account and in a local cache on your device.

Your AniList account

To use AniBinge you connect your AniList account through AniList's own secure sign-in page (OAuth). After you sign in, AniBinge receives an access token, which is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. The token is used solely to read and update your anime list (status, episode progress, scores) on AniList on your behalf. We never see your AniList password, and the token is never sent to any server we operate.

Your list data

Your AniList list (titles, watch status, episode progress, scores) is cached locally on your device — including in a shared app group container so that home-screen widgets and Live Activities can display it. This cached copy exists only on your device; the authoritative copy lives on AniList. AniBinge does not transmit your list to any third party.

Crash diagnostics

AniBinge uses Firebase Crashlytics (a service provided by Google) to collect crash reports and diagnostic information so we can find and fix bugs. These reports may include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and the technical stack trace of a crash. They do not include your AniList credentials, your access token, or the contents of your anime list. No advertising identifiers are collected and no App Tracking Transparency (ATT) tracking is performed. See Firebase's privacy information for how Google processes this data.

Episode metadata

To show episode titles and filler/recap information, AniBinge fetches metadata from the Jikan API (an unofficial MyAnimeList API). These requests contain only anime identifiers — no personal information about you is sent.

Data retention and deletion

To remove your data from the app, tap Log Out in Settings. This deletes the locally cached list and removes the stored AniList token from your device. To delete or change the underlying list or your account itself, manage it directly on AniList at anilist.co/settings.

Children

AniBinge is rated 17+ and is not directed to children under 13. Some content reachable through optional settings may be mature; mature content is disabled by default.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
