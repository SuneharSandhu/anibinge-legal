Common questions

I can't sign in. AniBinge requires a free AniList account (anilist.co). Sign in happens on AniList's own secure page. If login fails, make sure you can sign in at anilist.co, then try again.

How do I delete my data? Tap Log Out in Settings — this clears the local cache and removes the stored AniList token from your device. To manage or delete your AniList account itself, visit anilist.co/settings.

Restoring AniBinge Pro. Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.
